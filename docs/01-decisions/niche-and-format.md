# Niche and format

**What it is:** what your videos are about (niche) and what one video looks and sounds like (format).
Decide this first: every later choice depends on it.

## Niche

A niche works for automation when:

- **There's a large supply of material** you're allowed to use: public-domain stories, history, facts,
  your own expertise, data you collect. Running out after 20 videos is a common failure.
- **Accuracy can be checked.** Facts, history and science need sources; get them wrong and comments
  (and platforms' misinformation rules) will catch it.
- **It isn't built on other people's content.** Re-uploading clips, movies, TV, music, or other
  creators' videos breaks copyright and the platforms' "reused content" rules, and those channels are
  usually refused monetization.

## Format

Common faceless formats, all buildable with the tools in this guide:

| Format | On screen | Effort to automate | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Narrated story over images | Still images with slow pans/zooms, captions | Medium | Image licensing; images must match the scene |
| Narrated story over background footage | Gameplay or satisfying clips, captions | Low | Footage licence; "reused content" rules; low originality |
| Text or quote cards | Text on a background, maybe a voice | Low | Very crowded niche; easy to look generic |
| Facts or lists with visuals | Number + image per item, voice | Medium | Fact-checking every item |
| Data or charts | Animated charts, voice | High | Data source accuracy; rendering complexity |
| Series (multi-part stories) | Any of the above, split into parts | +Medium | Parts must be posted in order; each part must still make sense alone |

## Length

Shorts, TikToks and Reels are vertical (1080×1920). Short videos (about 30–60 seconds) are easiest to
keep watched to the end; longer is allowed on all platforms now but check the current limit per platform.

## Ask yourself

1. Can I name 50 video topics right now, and where does topic 500 come from?
2. Would I watch this video to the end if a stranger posted it? What makes it different from the
   ten channels already doing it?
3. Do I have the right to use every word, image, clip and sound in it?
